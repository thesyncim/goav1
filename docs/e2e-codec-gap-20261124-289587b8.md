# End-to-end codec gap: November 24, 2026 (evening, `289587b8`)

Measured at `289587b8` (main after #370) with official Go 1.27.4 and
`GOEXPERIMENT=simd` on Apple M4 Max (12 P + 4 E cores) / macOS 26.3.1, against
dav1d 1.5.4 (Homebrew) and SVT-AV1 4.1.0 built from the vendored source. Go
binaries were built with `-pgo=cmd/qualitybench/default.pgo`. This follows the
[morning report](e2e-codec-gap-20261124.md) (`5ceb50f0`) and covers the ~30 PRs
since (#340-#370). PR #372 (amd64 only) is not included.

**Machine load.** Two other agents were building and testing throughout. The
one-minute load average was **66-184** for every run in this report (the
morning report gated at <= 10). Instructions are load-independent and are the
primary comparison here; cycles are inflated by efficiency-core placement and
cache contention, by about 5% on the decoder against the morning's quiet
numbers, and threaded wall times are least reliable of all. Treat the cycle and
wall rows as provisional and re-run them on a quiet machine.

Method: process-inclusive CLI runs under `/usr/bin/time -l` (each command
`exec`'d, no shell wrapper), interleaved, minimum of five. goav1 decodes with
`-o /dev/null`, which still runs its YUV writer (`writeYUVFrame`, a buffered
plane copy). dav1d is shown with `--muxer null` (no copy, the reference row)
and with `--muxer yuv -o /dev/null` (the same copy goav1 makes). On the 10-bit
stream the writer is 121 Mcycles of goav1's profile, so the `--muxer yuv`
ratio is the like-for-like one.

## Decoder: where the single-thread gap is

Matched comparisons:

- **Reconstruction glue is level once matched.** goav1's `ReconB` +
  `reconCoefTree` + `reconTXB` + `addResidualInWindow` + `predictBlockBegin` +
  `reconBBegin` are about 320 Mcycles on aomrt_crowdrun; dav1d's
  `recon_b_inter` + `mc()` + `recon_b_intra` + `read_coef_tree` are about 312.
  The stage-table gap is classifier split, as in the morning report.
- **Loop filter: same structure, codegen gap.** The hottest LF lines of the
  vertical-edge `lpf8U8W` are the instructions after each `lpfAny` test
  (vector-to-GPR moves and the branch on fm, flat8in and the wd4 mask), which
  is mispredict and dependency-chain wait. dav1d's `lpf_16_wd*` makes the same
  tests the same way (`mov x16, v1.d[0]; mov x17, v1.d[1]; ... b.eq`), so
  there is no removable work there; the gap is register traffic in the
  transposes and phi moves.
- **Parse chain.** The largest single items outside the coefficient loop on
  aomrt_crowdrun: `decodeBlockPredictionModeInto` (~150), `txTree` (~145, vs
  dav1d `read_tx_tree` ~50 plus its bool reads),
  `decodeBlockLoopVisitWithCoeffControllerPtr` (~125), `mvComponentDiff`
  (~100), the `CoefBlock.DecodeCoefs` wrapper (~90, no dav1d counterpart),
  `decodeSymbol16SIMD` (~77), `DecodeInterMode` (~69). Each is a flat profile
  with no single hot line.
- **10-bit.** On svtrtc10_crowdrun the extra gap over the 8-bit stream comes
  from the HBD CDEF staging (`frameWorkAssembleCDEFInputHBDLE` +
  `loadSampleRows16` + `storeSampleRows16`, about 280 Mcycles: the unit is
  copied in, filtered into a uint16 buffer and copied back, where dav1d pads
  per block and writes the picture directly), the CLI writer (121 Mcycles of
  `memmove`), and HBD intra (`PredictIntraPlaneBlockWithExtentBounded` and
  edge prep, about 120 Mcycles against dav1d's ~60; #343 ported only the 8-bit
  edge prep).

## Ranked gaps

1. **Decoder parse chain: +656 / +257 Mcycles.** Flat. Proven lever:
   one-frame chains that keep the range state in registers (#338, #357) and
   removing call layers dav1d does not have. Landed below: the
   `CoefBlock.DecodeCoefs` layer. Tried and not landed: flattening
   `txTree`'s depth-limited recursion into one frame (byte-exact, but
   instructions moved -0.2% / +0.04%, not measurable).
2. **Motion compensation: +230 / +112.** Walled (USDOT 8-tap, no dot
   products in archsimd); recorded dead end.
3. **Loop filter: +227 on 8-tile content.** Same algorithm and branches as
   dav1d; the remainder is codegen (see above).
4. **10-bit CDEF staging: about +200 on 10-bit 1080p.** Addressable: port
   dav1d's 16bpc per-block padding and filter straight into the
   little-endian picture, dropping the unit copy-back
   (`frameWorkStoreCDEFBlocksHBD`, 49 Mcycles) and most of the unit copy-in.
5. **Loop-filter masks: +119.** Inside noise on one profile set; dav1d's
   `create_lf_mask_inter` does the same level and mask stores.
6. **HBD intra edge prep: about +60 on 10-bit.** Addressable: the 16bpc twin
   of #343.
7. **Encoder: the rest of the #364 regression, about +1.0G instructions
   (4%).** Addressable, safe: grid views like #368/#369 for the remaining
   encoder kernels (subpel bilinear, single-reference SADs, pred8, recon raw)
   and the `cdef`, `transform` and `tile` kernels the encoder calls. The
   pixel-stats grid version landed here recovers only a quarter of what the
   pointer version did (-0.08G against -0.33G); why is open. Beyond that, the
   remaining encoder stages are the SAD/variance ISA wall (UABD, UDOT)
   recorded in the morning report.

## Landed with this report

**Decode_coefs called straight from the transform-block walk.** dav1d's
`read_coef_tree` and `read_coef_blocks` call `decode_coefs` directly. goav1
went `reconTXB -> tile.(*CoefBlock).DecodeCoefs -> entropy.(*Reader).DecodeCoefs`,
and the middle frame (bounds checks, the inter chroma `txtp_map` lookup, the
luma `txtp_map` fill, packing a `CoefResult`) was a separate non-inlined
function: about 1.3% of decode cycles on aomrt_crowdrun and 0.55% on
svtrtc_crowdrun. `reconTXB` now does those steps itself and calls
`entropy.(*Reader).DecodeCoefs`; the tile package exposes `CoefEntropy`,
`TxtpMap`, `CoefSizeOf` and `FillTxtpMap` for it. Decode instructions at one
worker, min of 10: aomrt_crowdrun -0.42%, svtrtc_crowdrun -0.21%,
goav1_crowdrun -0.29%; cycles within noise at load 100+. Output is
byte-identical: realgate 42/42 at 1 and 8 workers, realcorpus10 5/5.

**Encoder kernels on unsafeutil grids (recovering #364).** Each kernel now
checks its source and reference footprints once as `unsafeutil` grids and
reads rows at constant columns of rows its loop bound proves, so no row or
window pays a check:

- `svtHMEColSumRow`: BenchmarkSVTHMEColSumRow 2862 ins/op before #364, 3287
  after, 2880 now;
- `svtAllSAD8x8_16x16Sub` (the SUB_SAD sweep): BenchmarkSweepSearchPoint8
  12415 before #364, 13322 after, 12540 now;
- `svtSAD16xhx8`, `svtSAD32xhx8` (`GridRowArray` gains `[23]` and `[39]`);
- `pixelStatsRows16Arch`.

Encoder instructions, rtc240, 1 Mbps, one thread: 27.02G -> 26.54G (-1.8%),
against 25.48G before #364. Output byte-identical (`ident.sh` at 0.5, 1 and
2 Mbps and 1 Mbps on 4 threads).
